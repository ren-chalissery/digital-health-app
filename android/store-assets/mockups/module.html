Introducing Simplicity to someone new

Section 3 of 5 · Privacy and consent

▶ Module video · 6:42

What to say about privacy

Simplicity helps someone track day-to-day wellbeing. Their reflections belong to them, and your training notes stay separate from the clinical record unless your service decides otherwise.

Before the first session, agree how you will use the app together, what you will look at together, and what stays private.
